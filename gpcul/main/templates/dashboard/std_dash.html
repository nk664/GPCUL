Frequently Asked Questions
How many books can I borrow at once?
You can borrow up to 5 books simultaneously. The limit is set by library policy for all students.
How do I renew a book?
Go to "Renew Books" from the sidebar or click the Renew button next to any active book. You can renew a book up to 2 times. Overdue books cannot be renewed.
What is the fine for overdue books?
The current fine rate is ₹20 per day per book after the due date. Fines can be paid at the library counter or through the online payment gateway.
How do I download my library card?
Go to "My Library Card" from the sidebar. You can download the card as a PDF or save it as an image. The QR code can be scanned at the library entrance.
I lost my library card. What should I do?
Contact the library counter immediately. A duplicate card can be issued for a fee of ₹50. Your digital card on this portal remains active.
